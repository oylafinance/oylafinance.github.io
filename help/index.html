Common questions

How do I add a card?

Tap the plus button on the Cards tab, or "Add a card" when the app is empty. The setup has three steps: the card (a nickname only you will see, the annual fee, the currency and the day its card year starts), its benefits, and reminders. On the second step you can start from ready-made sets, add benefits one by one, or copy them from a card you already added.

To look around first, tap "Try with a sample card" on the first step, or Settings > Data > Load sample card. The sample card can be removed in the same place.

What does "Typical value" mean?

Benefits added from a set come with round placeholder amounts. They are not taken from any card's terms. Each one is marked "Typical value" until you check it against your card and either confirm it or type the right amount.

My issuer ends a benefit on a different day than Cardwell shows

Cardwell works out each period from the reset rule you chose and your iPhone's calendar and time zone. A card issuer may close a period at a different hour, in its own time zone, or under terms that have changed since you set the benefit up. Treat your issuer's terms as the final word, adjust the benefit in Cardwell if needed, and use the benefit before the last day rather than on it.

Reminders do not arrive
  • In Cardwell, open Settings: "Remind me before benefits end" should be on and Permission should read "Allowed".
  • In iOS Settings > Notifications > Cardwell, notifications should be allowed. A Focus mode can also hold them back.
  • Each benefit has its own "Remind me before it ends" switch in its edit form.
  • "Send test reminder" in Settings shows a sample reminder about 5 seconds later.

iOS lets an app keep only a limited number of reminders scheduled ahead, so Cardwell plans the nearest ones and tops them up each time you open it. If it has not been opened for a long while, it sends "Reminders need a refresh"; opening the app is all that is needed.

Fewer reminders, or no amounts on the Lock Screen

Settings > Reminders has the time of day and a profile: Light sends fewer reminders, and later. Turning off "Show details in reminders and widgets" keeps benefit names and amounts out of notifications and widgets; they show only counts.

Adding the widgets

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, enter editing mode, add a widget and search for Cardwell; "Ending soon" comes in a small and a medium size. Widgets read a summary that Cardwell refreshes whenever you use the app. If a widget says "Open Cardwell to update", the app has not been opened for about two weeks; opening it once brings the widget up to date.

Moving to a new iPhone

Setting up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings Cardwell's data along. Without a backup: on the old iPhone choose Settings > Data > Save a copy and send the file to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop; on the new one choose Settings > Data > Restore from a copy and pick that file. Restoring replaces everything that is in Cardwell on the new iPhone, and the app asks before it does.

Getting my records into a spreadsheet

Settings > Data > Export usage (CSV) saves one calendar year of records as a CSV file that Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets can open. When you have records in more than one year, Cardwell asks which year. The sample card is never included.

Locking the app with Face ID

Turn on Settings > Privacy > App lock. Cardwell then asks for Face ID or your device passcode whenever you return to it and hides its screens in the app switcher. App lock needs a device passcode; without one, the switch stays off and the app says so.

Cardwell says it could not open its data

Nothing has been deleted: the file that would not open is kept on the iPhone. Choose Restore from a copy if you have saved one, or Start empty to carry on with a fresh start.

Deleting everything

Settings > Data > Delete all data removes every card, benefit and record after you confirm. Deleting the app from the iPhone removes everything, settings included. Copies you saved elsewhere and device backups are not touched; remove them where they are kept if you want them gone too.

Switching Cardwell between English and Russian

Cardwell's screens, reminders and widgets come in English and Russian. iOS uses whichever of the two stands higher in the language list under Settings > General > Language & Region, and English when neither is there. To run Cardwell in one of them while the rest of the iPhone stays in another, open Cardwell's page in the Settings app and change Language; that row appears once the language list holds more than one entry. Dates and amounts are written the way your region setting expects, whichever language is on.

Does Cardwell give financial advice?

No. Cardwell is a personal tracker. It does not connect to any bank or card account, does not hold or move money, and does not recommend cards or other financial products. The amounts and dates come from you, and the "covered" figure is simple arithmetic on them. Cardwell also stands apart from the rest of OYLA FINANCE's business: it shows no offers from the company and passes nothing to it.

A word about safety

Cardwell will never ask for a card number, a PIN, a password or a bank login, and it has no way to connect to an account. If a message, call or website asks you for such details in Cardwell's name, treat it as fraud and do not reply.
